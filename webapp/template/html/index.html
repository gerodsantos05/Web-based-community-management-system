Welcome to HappYness Project

What started as a simple desire to help has grown into a movement of care, compassion, and community. And we are just getting started.

Why Choose HappYness Project

A community-driven organization dedicated to uplifting lives — reaching families, communities, and institutions across the Philippines with care, support, and opportunity.

Academic Support

Helping children catch up to their grade level through guided learning and personalized attention.

Livelihood Training

Empowering mothers with practical skills and knowledge to build a sustainable source of income.

Community Outreach

Conducting close to 1,000 outreach activities reaching families and communities all over the Philippines.

Institutional Support

Extending help to hospitals, orphanages, public schools, elderly homes, and fire protection stations.

Essential Assistance

Providing meals, groceries, shoes, slippers, school supplies, and other basic necessities to those in need.

Communities Served

Making a real and lasting difference across indigent communities nationwide.
