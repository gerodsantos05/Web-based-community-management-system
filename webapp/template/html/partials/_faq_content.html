About the Organization

HappYness Project is a registered non-profit organization that aims to uplift marginalized communities-children, parents, and families-through holistic programs that support physical, social, economic, and spiritual development.

It began in 2018 and was officially registered as an NGO to continue the legacy of love, kindness, and compassion in honor of the founder's late mother, "Mama Sal," who was known for her generosity, kindness, and unwavering faith - always choosing to help others, even in the simplest ways. Her life and values serve as the inspiration behind HappYness Project's mission to spread love and serve communities in need.

We work closely with communities through proper community assessments, interviews, and profiling. We don't just provide one-time relief - we build sustainable programs that empower individuals and families.

We currently serve selected Barangays, communities, and institutions in Metro Manila and nearby provinces based on community needs and program partnerships. Our existing community engagements include Barangay Molino II, Bacoor, Cavite; Barangay 385, Quiapo, Manila; and Barangay Lunsad, Binangonan, Rizal.

Programs & Beneficiaries

We have three core programs: HappY Hearts - Empowerment and emotional well-being activities for senior citizens, special groups, and other vulnerable individuals. HappY Nanays - Livelihood and skills training to empower mothers and women. HappY Kids - Outreach programs, learning activities, feeding programs, and character-building activities for children.

Beneficiaries are selected based on actual community needs assessment, home visits and interviews, income level, and vulnerability status.
